# 🍽️ Zomato Restaurant Data Analysis

### 📊 Data Analytics Project | Alfido Tech

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Zomato restaurant data to understand restaurant ratings, popular cuisines, location preferences, pricing patterns, and factors associated with customer ratings.

Using Python and data visualization techniques, the project explores restaurant trends and provides data-driven business recommendations for a restaurant discovery platform.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze restaurant ratings and customer preferences.
* Identify the most popular cuisines.
* Explore restaurant hotspots across different locations.
* Investigate the relationship between restaurant pricing and ratings.
* Understand the influence of online ordering and table booking facilities.
* Visualize restaurant trends using graphs, heatmaps, and word clouds.
* Provide five business recommendations based on restaurant data.

## 📂 Dataset Information

**Dataset:** Zomato Restaurant Dataset

**Source:** [Kaggle – Zomato Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/bhanupratapbiswas/zomato)

The dataset contains information about restaurants, including:

* Restaurant names
* Locations
* Cuisines
* Customer ratings
* Customer votes
* Approximate cost for two people
* Restaurant types
* Online ordering availability
* Table booking availability
* Popular dishes

## 🧹 Data Cleaning and Preprocessing

The data preprocessing workflow includes:

1. Identifying and removing duplicate records.
2. Checking and handling missing values.
3. Cleaning restaurant names and other text fields.
4. Standardizing column names.
5. Converting restaurant ratings into numerical values.
6. Cleaning and converting restaurant prices.
7. Converting customer votes into numerical values.
8. Preparing cleaned data for analysis.

## 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis

The project covers the following analyses:

### 1. Restaurant Rating Distribution

Visualizing the distribution of customer ratings to understand overall restaurant performance.

### 2. Top 10 Restaurant Locations

Identifying locations with the highest concentration of restaurants.

### 3. Popular Cuisines

Analyzing frequently offered cuisines to understand restaurant availability patterns.

### 4. Cuisine vs Rating Analysis

Comparing average customer ratings across popular cuisines.

### 5. Price vs Rating Analysis

Investigating whether restaurant pricing is associated with customer ratings.

### 6. Correlation Heatmap

Visualizing relationships between restaurant ratings, customer votes, and approximate costs.

### 7. Cuisine Word Cloud

Creating a word cloud to highlight frequently occurring cuisines.

### 8. Online Ordering vs Ratings

Comparing customer ratings for restaurants with and without online ordering facilities.

### 9. Table Booking vs Ratings

Examining rating differences based on table booking availability.
